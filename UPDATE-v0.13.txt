GOOBS STUDIO v0.13 — VISUAL REBUILD / NEW GAMES 0.6 ALPHA
Changes-only GitHub Pages update over v0.12

INSTALL
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Upload its contents to the root of glennarmbruster/Goobs-Studio.
   Merge the assets, games and shared folders, replacing matching files.
   Keep all other repository files. This patch needs the existing v0.12 site.
3. Commit the upload and wait for GitHub Pages to deploy.
4. Open the site online. Use “New build ready · Reload” when offered.
   Confirm v0.13 on the launcher and wait for “Ready offline · all 10 games”.

The existing index.html is unchanged and is not included. The launcher changes
are in shared/app.js. Pages stays on main / root. No build command is required.
Saves and unlocks carry forward. Do not clear Safari site data.

WHAT CHANGED
Grand Orbital: illustrated playfield, cabinet, backbox and live score display;
chrome hardware, layered bumpers, animated lamps, tapered flippers and spring
plunger; an open orbit ramp with transparent floor and wire rails. Reframed for
portrait and landscape. Guide thickness, closed slingshot edges and tapered
flipper contacts now correspond more closely to their visible geometry.

Deepwater: detailed research sub, observation dome, hull bands, shrouded
thrusters/rotors, grabber arms and landing skids. Rebuilt wreck and research
dock, branching coral, kelp, fish and underwater light shafts. Local shadows
follow the submarine through the environment.

Harbor Architect: pitched roofs, shutters, gutters, framed windows, cafés with
striped awnings and seating, gardens, fountains, detailed solar panels and piers.
Added quayside scenery, marina equipment and modeled boats. Evening windows glow.

Outpost Command: irregular rocky islands, concrete bunkers, sandbag defenses,
communications equipment, detailed causeways and armored vehicles. Use
“View: Tactical / Close” to inspect the selected base. Vehicle pieces are batched
to reduce rendering overhead while retaining their detail.

Marble Odyssey: wooden plank decks, brass inlays, braces, shaped walls, toothed
gears with cutouts and a rebuilt portal. The conservatory now has glazing,
architectural details, garden planters and waterfalls. Track geometry continues
to follow the existing playable routes. Magnetic-pad lighting remains animated.

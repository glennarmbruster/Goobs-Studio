# Goobs Studio roadmap

## v0.5 — Expanded play (implemented)

- Fairway Coast: smaller ball, shorter rollout, stronger landing energy loss and putting retune.
- The Last Resort: reading lounge and engine workshop; four rooms across two linked chapters, with compass/antenna and pump/refueling puzzles.
- Diamond Nights: 3-, 6- and 9-inning matches, scorecards, selectable pitches, extra innings and walk-offs.
- Afterlight: orbital docks with life-support puzzle, alternating security gates, navigation core and launch sequence.
- Coastal Run: 200 mph / 322 km/h speed cap, saved units, speed-sensitive camera and revised steering/braking.
- Portrait/landscape layouts and offline play retained.

## Next foundation checks

- Use iPad feedback to tune ball visibility, golf stopping distances, baseball fielding and driving control at high speed.
- Check expanded rooms and sector progression on-device in both orientations.
- Profile frame rate, loading, memory and thermal behavior on the iPad Air M2 before claiming a verified performance target.

## Upcoming modes (not included in v0.5)

### Coastal Run

1. Traffic, with readable lane behavior and collision response.
2. Optional police chase: outrun the police.
3. Optional police pursuit: be the police and catch another driver.

Preserve the current relaxed coastal drive as an available mode.

### Diamond Nights

1. Home run derby with its own score and restart flow.
2. Batting practice with repeatable pitches and contact feedback.
3. Pitching practice with location and release challenges.

Keep these modes inside their existing games, behind the shared launcher.
